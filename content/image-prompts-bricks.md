# Prompts za Picha za Matofali — Zanzibar Bricks

Prompts hizi zinaweza kutumika kwenye DALL·E, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion au mfumo mwingine wa AI image generation. Tumia kwa **hero**, **backgrounds**, na **sehemu za bidhaa** kwenye tovuti.

---

## 1. Hero section (landscape, wide)

**English (recommended for most AI tools):**
```
Professional product photography of stacked sand-cement building bricks, light beige and sandy tan color, very rough porous granular texture with visible aggregates. Multiple bricks arranged in a clean construction setting on grey concrete surface. Soft natural daylight from the left, shallow depth of field. Tropical Zanzibar context suggested by soft warm tones. No people, no text. Wide horizontal composition 16:9, hero banner style, high resolution, photorealistic.
```

**Kiswahili (reference):**
Picha ya matofali ya ujenzi ya mchanga-simenti, rangi ya beige na tan, uso mgumu wenye mashimo na chembe. Matofali yamepangwa kwenye eneo safi la ujenzi. Mwanga wa asili, mtazamo wa pana (16:9) kwa hero.

---

## 2. Hero alternative — single brick, dramatic

**English:**
```
Close-up low-angle shot of one solid sand-cement brick, light beige color, extremely rough and porous surface with two vertical rounded grooves on the front face. Brick rests on smooth grey concrete floor. Soft diffused natural light from above-left, subtle shadows emphasizing texture. Construction or workshop environment, blurred background. Photorealistic, 8K, no people, no text. Sense of solidity and quality. Aspect ratio 16:9.
```

---

## 3. Product / pricing section background

**English:**
```
Overhead view of a single rectangular construction brick, light sandy beige, coarse granular porous texture, clean top surface with a subtle horizontal groove. Lying on plain light grey concrete floor. Even soft lighting, no harsh shadows. Minimal, clean product shot. No people, no shoes, no debris. Professional e-commerce style. Square or 4:3 aspect ratio.
```

---

## 4. Stack / quantity feel (for “bulk” or “order” areas)

**English:**
```
Stack of sand-cement building bricks, light beige and tan, rough textured surface. Bricks neatly stacked in rows on concrete ground. Natural daylight, construction site atmosphere but tidy. Suggests bulk supply and reliability. No people. Photorealistic, warm tones. 3:2 or 16:9.
```

---

## 5. Texture detail (for overlays or small sections)

**English:**
```
Extreme close-up of sand-cement brick surface only: rough, granular, porous texture, light beige with small grey and brown aggregate specks. Sharp focus, soft natural light showing depth. No full brick shape, just texture detail. Seamless tileable feel. Square format.
```

---

## 6. Tropical + construction (Zanzibar vibe for hero)

**English:**
```
Construction bricks, sand-cement, light beige, rough texture, stacked or scattered on clean ground. Soft tropical daylight, palm leaves or soft greenery very out of focus in background. Zanzibar coastal building vibe, professional and inviting. No people. Wide 16:9, photorealistic, warm color grading.
```

## Mwongozo wa matumizi

| Sehemu ya tovuti     | Prompt inayofaa |
|----------------------|------------------|
| Hero (background)    | 1 au 6           |
| Hero (alternative)   | 2                |
| Kando ya pricing     | 3                |
| Karibu na “Order”    | 4                |
| Overlay / texture    | 5                |

**Vipengele vya kushika kwenye picha zote:**
- **Rangi:** Light beige, sandy tan, grey-beige (sio rangi kali).
- **Texture:** Rough, porous, granular, aggregates visible — si laini.
- **Mazingira:** Construction / workshop / concrete floor; safi, bila viatu au vitu vya ziada.
- **Mwanga:** Soft natural light, diffuse, si glare kali.
- **Mtazamo:** Photorealistic, professional, hakuna watu na hakuna maandishi.

Unaweza kuongeza kwenye prompt: *“in the style of professional construction product photography”* au *“Zanzibar, East Africa”* ikiwa unataka mazingira zaidi ya eneo.
