# Njia za kuongeza SEO – Zanzibar Bricks

Tayari tumetumia: meta (title, description, keywords), Open Graph, Twitter Card, JSON-LD (LocalBusiness), canonical, sitemap, robots.txt.

## Zilizotumika sasa
- **sitemap.xml** – Google na wengine wanapata kurasa zote (/ na /order).
- **robots.txt** – Inaonyesha wavuti inaruhusiwa na mahali sitemap iko.

## Njia zingine unazoweza kutumia

### 1. Picha ya og-image (muhimu)
- Ongeza faili **og-image.jpg** (1200×630 px) kwenye `public/`.
- Inatumika unaposhare kiungo kwenye WhatsApp, Facebook, Twitter – inaongeza matokeo na ufikiaji.

### 2. Picha zote na alt text
- Kila `<img>` lazima iwe na `alt` yenye maneno maelezo (k.m. “Vikuku vya tofali 5 inch Zanzibar”).
- Inasaidia watafutaji na watumiaji wenye matatizo ya kuona.

### 3. Majina ya kurasa (heading) sahihi
- Ukurasa kuu uwe na **H1** moja tu (k.m. “Zanzibar Bricks”).
- Vitengo vya maudhui vye H2, ndani yake H3 ikiwa kuna vichwa vidogo – usiruke kiwango (H1 → H3 bila H2).

### 4. Schema za bidhaa (Product / Offer)
- Unaweza kuongeza JSON-LD ya bidhaa (tofali 4", 5", 6") na bei kwa maeneo – inasaidia matokeo ya “brick price Zanzibar” na rich results.

### 5. Udomo na ufikiaji (Core Web Vitals)
- Hakikisha tovuti inapakia haraka: picha zilizo optimized, lazy loading.
- Tumia Google Search Console “Core Web Vitals” kuangalia na kuboresha.

### 6. Maudhui ya ziada (blog / kurasa)
- Kurasa kama “Maeneo tunayotumilia” au “Vidokezo kwa ujenzi” zinaweza kuongeza maneno muhimu na kurasa zinazofaa kwa Google.

### 7. Google Search Console na Bing
- Ingiza wavuti kwenye [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) na [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters).
- Ingiza sitemap: `https://bricks.zanzibaba.com/sitemap.xml`.

### 8. Viungo vya nje na ndani
- Viungo kutoka tovuti nyingine (k.m. zanzibaba.com) kwenda bricks.zanzibaba.com vinaongeza “trust”.
- Viungo vya ndani (k.m. kutoka ukurasa kuu kwenda /order) yanasaidia Google kugundua kurasa.

### 9. HTTPS na kasi
- Hakikisha kikoa kina SSL (https) – tayari unatumia bricks.zanzibaba.com.
- Hosting yenye CDN karibu na Tanzania inaweza kuongeza kasi.

### 10. Hreflang (ikiwa utatenga EN/SW)
- Ikiwa utafanya kurasa tofauti kwa Kiingereza na Kiswahili (k.m. /en na /sw), ongeza meta `hreflang` ili Google ajue lugha ya kila ukurasa.

---

**Kwa sasa:** sitemap na robots zimeongezwa. Ongeza og-image.jpg, uingize tovuti kwenye Search Console, na uendelea kuboresha maudhui na picha (alt text).
